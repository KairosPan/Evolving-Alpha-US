05 / A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A few questions,
answered.

What the product does, and where it stands.

What is Gravit?+

Gravit is an AI workbench for individual investment research. It brings market observation, strategy research, multiple perspectives and account views into one environment for organizing evidence, testing ideas and preserving the work.

Does it make investment decisions or execute trades?+

The final judgment is yours. Kairos helps organize evidence, compare views and advance the research. The account functionality shown here is read-only and does not execute trades.

Are the quotes, discussions and accounts real?+

This tour uses illustrative content. Prices and account balances are fictional, and the discussions are examples. The four views demonstrate the workflow without connecting to live feeds or real accounts.

Can I use the full workbench online?+

This site is a product introduction and interactive tour. The full workbench runs locally with a configured model and data sources. Online signup and hosted access are not yet available.
