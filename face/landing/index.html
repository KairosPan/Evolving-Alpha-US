05 / A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A few questions,
answered.

What the product does, and where it stands.

What is Gravit?+

Gravit is an AI workbench for individual investment research. It brings market observation, strategy research, multiple perspectives and account views into one environment for organizing evidence, testing ideas and preserving the work.

Does it make investment decisions or execute trades?+

The final judgment is yours. Gravit helps organize evidence, compare views and advance the research. The account functionality shown here is read-only and does not execute trades.

Are the research records, quotes and accounts real?+

The three screenshots show the actual product interface with staged sample data and English labels adapted for this tour. Prices, account balances and research records are illustrative. No live feed or real account is connected to this page.

Can I use the full workbench online?+

This site is a product introduction with a screenshot tour. The full workbench runs locally with a configured model and data sources. Online signup and hosted access are not yet available.
