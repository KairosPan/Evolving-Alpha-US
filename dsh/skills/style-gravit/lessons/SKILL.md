# Lessons — operator failure signatures

> **Scope: operator style, not market law.** These are the operator's personal
> investment rules and preferences. Follow them by default — but when research findings
> conflict with an entry here, REPORT the conflict; do not silently defer.


## panic_rebound_not_leaders (principle)
- **Failure:** bought old leaders in a post-panic rebound
- **Analog:** post-freeze recovery-leader chase (inverted)
- **Phases:** market:panic_state
- **Lesson:** After a panic, the rebound is led by beaten-down losers, not the old leaders; wait for a new base, a new follow-through, and a new leadership list before buying strength.

## public_laggard_is_a_clock (principle)
- **Failure:** chased laggard catch-up as a fresh entry
- **Analog:** catch-up / lateral extension buying (inverted)
- **Phases:** theme:public_laggard
- **Lesson:** Laggards launching en masse is the theme's public-laggard timer, not a buy signal; in the public phase it is the momentum-crash catch-up trap.

## divergence_to_consensus_is_a_top (principle)
- **Failure:** bought a high-volume stall expecting one more leg after consensus
- **Analog:** divergence-then-consensus (inverted)
- **Phases:** stock:top
- **Lesson:** At the weekly scale the 'divergence-to-consensus for one more leg' read is a day-trade artifact; here it coincides with a distribution top — high-volume stalling is the exit.

## leader_holds_theme_lives (principle)
- **Failure:** stayed in a theme after its leader broke down on volume
- **Analog:** leader stands, theme lives
- **Phases:** stock:decline
- **Lesson:** A leader breaking key moving averages on volume is the number-one signal of a theme cycle turning; re-read the theme clock before defending the stock.

## industry_chain_readthrough (principle)
- **Analog:** supply-chain read-through
- **Phases:** theme:institutional
- **Lesson:** One link's earnings forecast the next link's orders; a customer's capex guide is the supplier's revenue lead — the cleanest positive transfer, run on a quarterly timescale.

## five_confluences (principle)
- **Failure:** sized a heat spike with no industry confluence as a real theme
- **Analog:** five confluences
- **Phases:** theme:emerging
- **Lesson:** A theme's size is its confluence count — industry fact, institutional consensus, public participation, policy tailwind, cross-market confirmation; heat with zero confluence is a chip game.

## pre_registered_surprise (principle)
- **Analog:** expectations gap
- **Lesson:** The edge is a pre-registered fundamental surprise from industry insight, not anomaly arbitrage; register what the world will show first, before it does.

## cash_is_a_position (principle)
- **Failure:** forced trades in a correction
- **Analog:** sit out and rest
- **Phases:** market:correction
- **Lesson:** In a correction, cash is a position; no new entries, only reduce.

## plan_the_trade (principle)
- **Analog:** plan the trade
- **Lesson:** The thesis card is the plan; trade the plan, not the tape.

## hold_via_the_matrix (principle)
- **Failure:** held on willpower after the thesis broke
- **Analog:** conviction to hold
- **Lesson:** Conviction to hold comes from the thesis-price matrix, not willpower; a good price on a broken thesis is an exit, not a reason to stay.

## connate_new_leaders (principle)
- **Analog:** new-core / new leadership
- **Phases:** market:panic_state
- **Lesson:** The new cycle's leaders are usually not the last cycle's; after a panic, watch for the new leadership list rather than the old names.

## disprovable_assumption (loss)
- **Failure:** thesis core assumption had no falsifiable verification node
- **Analog:** Peloton 2021 — demand pull-forward vs behavior shift
- **Lesson:** Distinguishable assumptions (demand pull-forward vs permanent behavior shift) must be written as explicit verification nodes, or two opposite theses look identical in a bull market — the counter-thesis was the truth.

## thesis_card_template (principle)
- **Analog:** thesis card
- **Lesson:** Template for every invested theme, four prose answers: (1) the thesis — name customers, competitors, bottlenecks, no market language; (2) why now — the inflection; (3) the smart short case; (4) what the world shows first — a pre-registered surprise with calendar-anchored verification nodes.

## sentiment_cycle_fractalizes (principle)
- **Analog:** sentiment cycle -> three-scale clock
- **Lesson:** The old single-scale daily sentiment cycle is not discarded but fractalized into three nested clocks — market state (read daily), theme lifecycle (weekly), stock stage (weekly); a cycle word is meaningless until you name which scale it belongs to.

## wealth_effect_is_breadth (principle)
- **Analog:** wealth effect / loss effect
- **Phases:** market:confirmed_uptrend, market:under_pressure, market:correction
- **Lesson:** The felt money-making vs money-losing effect migrates to computable breadth — % above the 200-day line, net new 52-week highs, advance/decline — feeding the three market states; measure it, do not eyeball it.

## leader_is_profit_bearer (principle)
- **Failure:** ranked a strong chart with no industry-profit case as the leader
- **Analog:** leader / head-dragon
- **Phases:** theme:institutional, stock:advance
- **Lesson:** A leader is industry-profit bearer x market confirmation, both required; candidates come only from the thesis card's profit-attribution chain, never from price strength alone — confirmation without profit is a chip game, profit without confirmation a value trap.

## leader_breakdown_drags_the_theme (principle)
- **Failure:** held the theme after its leader broke down and dragged the whole group
- **Analog:** leader breakdown drags the sector
- **Phases:** stock:top, stock:decline
- **Lesson:** The A-share limit-down leader that drags its sector has no US structural analog; it migrates to a leader breaking a key moving average on volume — the number-one signal the whole theme is turning, forcing a theme-clock re-read before defending any name in the group.

## rotation_acceleration_is_the_endgame (principle)
- **Failure:** chased accelerating rotation as fresh opportunity instead of the tail
- **Analog:** rotation acceleration = endgame
- **Phases:** theme:exhaustion
- **Lesson:** Accelerating rotation — fringe names dancing, delivery no longer moving price — is the theme's exhaustion criterion, the tail end of the cycle, not a fresh opportunity to chase.

## refusing_to_weaken_by_scale (principle)
- **Failure:** migrated the intraday weak-turns-strong read to the weekly horizon
- **Analog:** weak-turns-strong (refusing to weaken)
- **Phases:** stock:advance
- **Lesson:** 'Refusing to weaken is strength' splits by scale: the intraday weak-turns-strong read is a tombstone — a T+1 day-trade artifact, do not migrate; at the weekly scale it survives transformed — a fast reclaim after a shakeout, undercut-and-rally, or an earnings gap-up that does not fill are institutions supporting — but it only confirms an existing thesis candidate, never discovers a new one.

## insider_selling_is_an_exit_signal (principle)
- **Analog:** insider selling -> exit
- **Lesson:** The A-share reflex 'when insiders cut, you run' migrates to US insider-and-dilution alerts — Form 4 sales, ATM offerings, S-3 shelf registrations; the discipline formalizes into a rule once the data feed lands (deferred to P5 feeds).

## topping_pattern_is_stage_language (principle)
- **Failure:** read a candlestick topping pattern as the signal instead of the stage
- **Analog:** topping pattern
- **Phases:** stock:top
- **Lesson:** Candlestick topping patterns (evening star and kin) migrate from chart-shape reading to stock-stage top language: a climax run far above the moving averages, or high-volume stalling and distribution — this is trim language, not add language.
