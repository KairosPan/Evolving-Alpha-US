The bench

Three layers, one book

Market

alpaca_kit · point-in-time

US equities, Alpaca + EDGAR, captured into offline point-in-time beds. Every dated read passes a lookahead guard before it reaches an eye or a backtest.

Strategy

strategies/ · the agent’s arena

A strategy is a git-versioned directory: a thesis with falsification conditions, an executable screen, backtests, a journal, a lifecycle state. Kairos writes here freely; git is the ledger.

Account

alpaca_kit · paper · read-only

An Alpaca paper account, read-only today. Order capability exists in code and is double-gated; no order reaches any market through a gate Kairos can open.

Standing entries

Five principles, entered once

from the charter · Kairos-Design.md §2
P1entered

Wide hands, no self-keys

The agent’s reach is wide — shell, code, market tools, the whole arena. Its authority over its own runtime is zero: profile, credentials and gate flags live outside its workspace.

placement, not rules
P2entered

The operator is the only teacher

Trading style is an operator-owned pack. Kairos follows it, and when research disagrees with style it reports the conflict — never silently defers, never silently overrides.

conflicts surface
P3entered

Point-in-time honesty, in code

Every dated market read passes a lookahead guard, and the guard surfaces are pinned by name in tests. A backtest that cannot be honest fails loudly rather than succeeding approximately.

guards pinned by tests
P4entered

Honest evaluation

Five backtest rules bind every experiment: PIT channel only; a delisting during a hold is a terminal loss; returns are gross and say so; no same-day round trip; missing data is counted, never fabricated.

five binding rules
P5entered

The human is the steady state

No autonomy ladder, no graduation. Operator attention is the scarcest resource and the binding rate limit — economized, never designed away.

no autonomy ladder
