The charter

Five principles, engraved.

P·Iplacement

Wide hands, no self-keys.

The action space is deliberately large — shell, code, market tools, the whole arena. Authority over its own runtime: zero. Enforced by placement, not by rules: profile, credentials and gate flags live outside the workspace.

P·IIteaching

The operator is the only teacher.

Trading style lives in an operator-owned pack. When research conflicts with a style entry, Kairos reports the conflict — never silently defers, never silently overrides.

P·IIIlookahead

Point-in-time honesty, enforced in code.

Every dated market read passes a lookahead guard, its surfaces pinned by name in tests. A backtest that cannot be honest fails loudly rather than succeeding approximately.

P·IVevidence

Honest evaluation.

Five backtest rules bind every experiment. A delisting mid-hold is a terminal loss; missing data is discarded and counted, never fabricated; every cited number states what was actually measured.

P·Vsteady state

The human is the steady state.

No autonomy ladder, no graduation criteria. Operator attention is the system's binding rate limit — economized everywhere, designed away nowhere.
